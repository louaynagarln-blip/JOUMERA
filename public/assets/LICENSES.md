# Integrated asset licences

Seven actual Poly Haven models and one three-map PBR marble set are included in this directory. The files are CC0-1.0, permitting commercial use and redistribution. Source: https://polyhaven.com/license (checked 2026-10-21). CC0 summary: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

- modern_arm_chair_01: seating in atrium, HOME GALLERY and STYLE LAB
- ceramic_vase_03: display niches and interactive HOME GALLERY demo
- potted_plant_02: planted arrival, atrium and shop corners
- modern_ceiling_lamp_01: suspended shop lighting fixtures
- classic_laptop: TECH STUDIO heritage display and interactive demo; not a modern retail laptop
- sofa_03: HOME GALLERY lounge vignette
- modern_coffee_table_01: HOME GALLERY lounge vignette
- marble_01: albedo, OpenGL normal and roughness maps on architectural stone

These are decorative or explicitly labelled demonstration assets, not supplier-verified sellable inventory. No website example renders or product photography were copied. The architecture remains custom procedural geometry; no complete professionally authored mall environment is supplied.

`PROVENANCE.json` records source download URLs, source MD5/size checks, final SHA256, triangle counts and exact optimisation options. Models are self-contained GLB with Meshopt geometry and embedded 512px WebP textures. WebP reduces download size, not GPU texture memory; KTX2/Basis compression and baked lighting have not been implemented.
